Candidate claims

Not approved. Each candidate is built from one "This claim dies if …" sentence shared by one or more Kunskapsbank notes. The kill sentence is quoted in Swedish, verbatim; the claim sentence and English kill condition are first drafts. Candidates have no ledger and are not in the brief. Only Jonatan promotes a candidate. K01 and K12 were promoted on 2026-09-25 as C3 and C2.

K02 candidate: not approved

Giving every pupil their own computer or tablet (1:1) does not raise average knowledge results in Swedish schools under realistic use.

Serves: Q2 · Ages: foralder-tidig-barndom, grundskola-hog, grundskola-lag, gymnasium, overgripande

What would kill this: A Swedish/Nordic difference-in-differences study or RCT in which 1:1 devices under realistic use raise closed-book knowledge without more distraction.

Kill sentence in the bank (Swedish): Svensk/nordisk DiD eller RCT där 1:1-enheter under realistisk användning höjer closed-book-kunskap utan ökad distraktion.

Overlap: none noted
